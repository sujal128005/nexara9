<section class="section alt" id="how">
  <div class="wrap">
    <h2 class="section-title center" data-i18n="how.title">The AI reads. The maths is done by code.</h2>
    <p class="section-sub center" data-i18n="how.sub">Language models are good at reading and bad at arithmetic. So we never let them do the arithmetic.</p>
    <div class="steps">
      <div class="step">
        <span class="step-num">1</span>
        <h3 data-i18n="how.1.title">It reads</h3>
        <p data-i18n="how.1.text">An AI model pulls out every term, and it has to quote the exact words from your document for each one. A second, rule-based reader checks the same document independently.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="step">
        <span class="step-num">2</span>
        <h3 data-i18n="how.2.title">It checks</h3>
        <p data-i18n="how.2.text">Quotes that are not in the document are thrown out. The verified terms go into a calculation engine that works out the true yearly cost, then into 21 checks based on RBI and IRDAI rules.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="step">
        <span class="step-num">3</span>
        <h3 data-i18n="how.3.title">It explains</h3>
        <p data-i18n="how.3.text">You get the result in plain words, in 12 Indian languages, with questions to ask and a message you can send back. Any number in that text is matched against the calculation.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
