## By minute

**Minute 1 (0:00 to 0:59)**

Hi, we are team ArthaNetra. Every loan, EMI or insurance offer shows you one number. ArthaNetra shows you the one you will actually pay. Take this loan app. It says 0.1% a day. That sounds cheap. The real cost is 807% a year. You can paste any offer, upload a PDF or a screenshot, or try one of our examples. Let's check the instant loan app. In a few seconds, our AI reads the fine print, checks every number, and calculates the true cost. Trap Score: 100 out of 100. Predatory. The app shows 36.5% a year. What you really pay is 807%, more than 22 times what they show. Follow the money. You are sanctioned 5,000 rupees, but only 3,938 reaches your account, and you repay 5,075 in just 15 days.

**Minute 2 (1:00 to 1:59)**

ArthaNetra also checks the offer against 21 RBI and IRDAI rules. This app wants your contacts and photos, threatens to call your family, compounds penalty interest, and gives no cooling-off period. Every point shows its source. Here is why you can trust it. The AI must quote the exact words from the document for every number. Click any row, and it is highlighted. Now let's negotiate. If the lender drops the upfront fee, the true cost falls from 807% to about 37%. Now you know exactly what to ask for. Everything is explained in plain words, with questions to ask before signing and a message you can send back to the lender. ArthaNetra speaks 12 Indian languages. Let's switch to Hindi, and check a WhatsApp message from an insurance agent who promises double money, guaranteed.

**Minute 3 (2:00 to 2:59)**

The pitch claims 12% returns. The real return is just 3.26% a year, less than a PPF account. And the full explanation is in Hindi, for the families these pitches target most. You can also ask your own question, by typing or speaking. We ask: is this really guaranteed? The answer: no. A market-linked plan cannot guarantee returns. Because this is about money, privacy comes first. PAN, Aadhaar and phone numbers are masked before the AI sees anything, and nothing is stored. And when an offer is honest, ArthaNetra says so. Let's check a loan from a co-operative bank. Trap Score 2. Fair. The disclosed rate matches our calculation, and there is a proper cooling-off period. ArthaNetra. So that no one signs a price they cannot see.

## How to add your voice

1. Open the video in Clipchamp (built into Windows 11), CapCut, or any editor.
2. Use the editor's voice-over or record button and read the script while the video plays. Do it in one take or
   line by line.
3. Optionally add `ArthaNetra_Demo_captions.srt` as subtitles (judges often watch without sound).
4. Export at 1080p and upload to YouTube (unlisted) or Google Drive.

## Tips

- Use earphones with a mic and record in a quiet room.
- Smile while you speak, it sounds warmer.
- Say the numbers clearly: "eight hundred and seven percent", "three point two six percent".
