at KL University.

Both of IIITDM Kurnool.

## How we worked

We worked sequentially on one laptop, swapping the keyboard when the person
typing started making mistakes. The person not typing tested, wrote
documentation, and played the customer during rehearsals.

Both of us contributed to the architecture, the design decisions, and the
debugging. The commit history reflects a single machine rather than a division
of the work — every decision recorded in this repository was made by both of us.

## The parts we are proudest of

The deterministic verification engine, which keeps the language model out of the
verification path entirely. The self-check loop, which re-parses and re-checks
the model's own drafted corrections before a human sees them. And the coverage
guard, which came out of catching our own system reporting "no misquotes
detected" on a call where the rep had misquoted twice.
